Our Vision

We are embarking on a transformative journey to construct a state-of-the-art school. This project will offer modern classrooms, science and computer labs, and a vibrant library that inspires learning and growth.

Location: Ruiru, Kiambu, Kenya

Project Details

The new school will not only provide quality education but also create a nurturing environment for our future leaders. With spacious classrooms, innovative learning spaces, and dedicated teachers, we aim to spark creativity and academic excellence.

We kindly appeal to donors to support the construction of a school within our children’s home, a transformative project aimed at reducing the high costs of external education while ensuring quality, consistent learning for the children under our care. Establishing an on-site school will not only provide a stable and nurturing environment for our children but also extend educational opportunities to vulnerable children in the surrounding community, fostering long-term social and economic impact. By investing in this initiative, donors will help us create a self-sustaining model that promotes education, empowerment, and community upliftment.

Your contribution, no matter the size, will make a lasting difference.
